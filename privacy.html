Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

MyKit is a personal, non-commercial Android app. It has no servers of its own, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer never receives any of your data.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, libraries, reading and watching progress, feeds, countdowns, workouts and installed extensions are stored only in MyKit's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, MyKit asks only for what a feature needs:

  • YouTube (read-only) — youtube.readonly, to import the list of channels you're subscribed to so they can be added as feeds. MyKit never posts, subscribes or changes anything on YouTube.
  • Google Drive app data — drive.appdata, to save MyKit backups in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only MyKit can use. MyKit cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.

The sign-in token is stored only on your device. Data from Google APIs goes straight between your phone and Google, is used only for these features, and is never sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising. MyKit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove MyKit's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting MyKit's backups: Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → MyKit → Delete hidden app data.

Services you choose to use

Some features talk to third-party services directly from your phone, only when you use them: the RSS feeds, add-ons, extensions and sources you add yourself, and — if you enter your own API key — Google Gemini for article summaries (the article text is sent to Google for that). Those services have their own privacy policies.
